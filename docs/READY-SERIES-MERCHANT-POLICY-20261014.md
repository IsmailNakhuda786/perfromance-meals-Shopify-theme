# Ready Series merchant-confirmed prepaid policy

Jerome's supplied reply supersedes the earlier non-renewing interpretation.

- The approved 3- and 6-month terms are paid in full upfront.
- Delivery is once monthly: 3 or 6 deliveries per paid term.
- The customer selects a delivery date, which repeats until changed in their account.
- The full selected term renews automatically unless cancelled.
- Prices and contents come from the merchant package sheets; GST is included.
- Unavailable contents require customer contact for replacement approval.

The local configuration auditor requires billing MONTH × 3/6, delivery MONTH × 1 and no fixed billing-cycle cap. It rejects monthly instalments and the obsolete one-cycle cap. It does not establish native payment capability, a valid contract, verified prices, date anchoring, scheduled fulfillment or cancellation eligibility. Purchase guards remain closed until those independent checks pass.

Do not infer a shorter-month, weekend or public-holiday rule. Those calendar details, cutoff interpretation and changes to already paid terms remain pending. No native selling plan, payment provider, contract or customer balance was changed by this correction.

# Customer and Core completion tracker — public summary

The original development workspace retains the detailed private QA evidence.
This public snapshot deliberately excludes customer-specific order/refund/balance
records and private operational QA reports. Tests use synthetic fixtures only.

Final client policy: Support reviews cancellation/refund requests; customers
cannot automatically cancel/refund orders. Ready Series prepaid terms and both
60-day programmes are non-renewing. Recurring Meal Plan contract management
remains subject to native subscription verification.

Rewards: original customer-paid food only; default 1 point per SGD, cents ignored.
Delivery, Gift Card/Reward/bonus funding, Gift Card purchases and top-ups excluded.
Promotions replace regular rates without stacking; redemption is 10 points per SGD.
Persistent configuration, points history, redemption operations, Support requests
and Wallet funding attribution are included in this source snapshot. Native
end-to-end earning/redemption and authorized Support refund execution remain open.
Rewards and top-ups remain disabled pending the documented acceptance gates.

The project tracks fifteen functional requirements. No missing fourteen-point
acceptance checklist is invented. Admin UI development and production release
remain outside this checkpoint. Original source history is not included.
